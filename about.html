The short version

I'm Ariz Karim, a frontend developer trained at SMIT. I treat UI as seriously as logic — the spacing, the hierarchy, the way a hover feels, whether the page holds up at 360px.

I pair that design discipline with clean, vanilla JavaScript, so the sites I build don't just work — they feel considered. That's what you get when you hire me: a developer who thinks about your customers, not just the code.

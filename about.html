About

Design-first, code-backed.

I build interfaces that are as considered as they are functional — here's how I approach the work.

The short version

I'm Ariz Karim, a junior frontend developer in Karachi, trained at SMIT. I treat UI as seriously as logic — the spacing, the hierarchy, the way a hover feels, whether the page holds up at 360px.

I pair that design discipline with clean, vanilla JavaScript, so the sites I build don't just work — they feel considered. That's what you get when you work with me: a developer who thinks about your customers, not just the code. I build sites for small businesses and independent brands, and I'm also open to junior roles and internships.
